POST-DEFENSE SCOPE REVISION: LIVESTOCK, POULTRY, AND FISHERIES

Source of change:
Not a panel comment. After the defense the stakeholder asked that the system cover livestock
and poultry raising, municipal fisheries, and aquaculture in addition to crops, since the
Office of the Municipal Agriculturalist is responsible for municipal agriculture as a whole.

Compliance:
The manuscript and the system were both revised for the three domains.

Manuscript — Chapter I introduction, framework and IPO, statement of the problem, objectives,
scope and delimitations, significance, output, and definition of terms now cover the added
sectors. Chapter II gained sections on integrated crop, livestock, and fisheries information
systems, livestock and poultry records management, and fisheries and aquaculture records
management, with references to Philippine Statistics Authority, BFAR, and FAO sources.
Chapter III updated the research setting, data gathering, current and proposed system flow,
architecture, data dictionary, and use cases. The software requirements were corrected from
MySQL and a standalone Node.js runtime to PostgreSQL with Supabase, which is what the system
actually uses.

System — added the animal species reference list, livestock and poultry records, fisherfolk
profiles, fish catch records, aquaculture sites, and culture cycles, together with the
livestock and fisheries statistical summaries. The existing farmers table serves as the
unified producer registry so one person may hold crop, livestock, fisheries, and aquaculture
records without duplicate identities.

Scope limits kept explicit: no forecasting or predictive analytics, no veterinary diagnosis
or disease surveillance, no fisheries licensing, enforcement, or vessel tracking, and no
direct producer access to the system.
